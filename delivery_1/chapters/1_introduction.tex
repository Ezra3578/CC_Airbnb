\section{Introduction}

In the present document, it will be boarded the system description of Airbnb, 
which will the appropiate analysis of its business problem, which actors take part of it,
describe its main Use Cases, and define the boundaries within the system, i.e., what are the limits 
of the designed and development of the system.

Then, it will be redacted and described the Functional and Non-Functional Requirements that the system 
accomplishes in a real-life situation. The values of the Non-Functional Requirements, will be defined using 
Airbnb Workload Model, which will appear in its own section in the document.

The boundaries and limits, will also specify how will the real Non-Functional Requirements be taken into 
account for its simulation.
