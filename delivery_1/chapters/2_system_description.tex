\subsection{Business Problem}

The problem that Airbnb solves is referred to the fragmented, inefficient and uncertain access to short-term 
accomodations. It gives people who are in need of a place to stay, and people with those accomodations or 
properties available. The hosts might find problems finding or reaching potential guests, managing bookings 
and monetizing after all the stay, and guests might struggle finding said place.

Airbnb provides a platform in which guests are benefited with a large range and access of accomodation options, and hosts
can get additional income and a wider spectre of possible guests around the world.

Its operational challenge at scale, comes when one must maintain availibility accuracy (concurrence), transaction integrity,
trust, and service reliability accross a global two-sided marketplace. Also, there might be some problems taking compliance
into account.
