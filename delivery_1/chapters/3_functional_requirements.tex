\section{Functional Requirements}

In this section, there are presented 10 functional requirements describing what the system must do. They are focused 
to fulfill all of the required characteristics. 


\subsection{Rental Management}

Here are described some funtional requirements related to rental management.


\begin{longtable}{|p{0.17\textwidth}|p{0.75\textwidth}|}
    \hline
    \textbf{ID} & 
    \textbf{Functional Requirement} \\
    \hline
    \endfirsthead

    \hline
    \textbf{ID} & \textbf{Functional Requirement} \\
    \hline
    \endhead

    FR01 & 
    The system shall allow hosts to publish rental properties by providing the required property information, 
    pricing, availability dates, and usage conditions. \\
    \hline

    FR02 &
    The system shall allow hosts to manage their published rental properties by modifying their property information, 
    pricing, availability, and usage conditions while preserving existing reservations. \\
    \hline

    FR03 &
    The system shall calculate the rental price for a reservation according to the configured pricing rules and 
    applicable dynamic rates for the selected dates. \\
    \hline

    FR04 &
    The system shall allow guests to select rental dates and the number of guests before creating a reservation, 
    subject to the property's configured availability and capacity. \\
    \hline

    FR05 &
    The system shall record check-in and check-out agreements for each confirmed reservation before the reservation 
    is completed. \\
    \hline

    FR06 &
    The system shall allow hosts to define specific conditions for guests, including guest capacity and additional 
    conditions associated with extra charges, before publishing a rental property. \\
    \hline

    \caption{Rental Management Requirements}

    \label{tab:rm_fr}

\end{longtable}
