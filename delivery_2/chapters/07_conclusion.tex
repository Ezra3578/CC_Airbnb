\section{Conclusion}
\label{ch:reflection}

The architecture separates transactional workflows (booking, payment,
identity) from read-heavy discovery and from side effects such as
notifications, indicators and audit indexing. Exclusive data ownership,
synchronous calls only where the user waits, and events delivered at least
once to idempotent consumers balance correctness and responsiveness. Booking
and payment remain an explicit saga because the platform and the external
gateway cannot share an atomic transaction.

The design is cloud-independent and will be mapped to infrastructure in
Delivery~3. Before implementation the team must validate the API contracts,
finalize the payment and hold state machine, define retention and privacy
rules, and confirm the capacity estimates with load tests.
