\section{System Context Diagram}

\subsection{System Boundary}

The system contains account access, accommodation publication and discovery,
reservations, messaging, support, administration and audit. Payment
processing, maps and geolocation, and the delivery of external notifications
are external systems. Delivery~1 also showed an external authentication
service; in this design authentication is internal, and an external identity
provider could be added later without changing the service boundaries.
Figure~\ref{fig:systemcontextdiagram} shows the system as a black box together
with the users and external systems identified in Delivery~1.
